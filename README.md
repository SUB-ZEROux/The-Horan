## Overview

**THE HORAN** is a dark, cinematic science-fiction novel concept exploring power, guilt, and the terrifying cost of civilization. In a distant future, a fallen architect from the advanced empire of ESKAS arrives on Earth with a mission that could reshape the destiny of the entire planet.

But when he discovers something hidden beneath the surface of this world, he is forced to confront a question no civilization has ever answered honestly:

What is the price of survival when the survival of one species means the destruction of another?

## Story Premise

The empire of ESKAS is not merely advanced — it is godlike. It has conquered worlds, harvested the resources of entire galaxies, and treated planets as expendable variables in a vast cosmic equation.

Their most valuable resource is Exlir, a rare mineral that powers time itself.

When one of ESKAS’s most feared emissaries, Horan, begins to question the morality of his civilization’s relentless expansion, he discovers a secret buried beneath Earth that could either save humanity or ignite the destruction of everything he once believed was right.

Now, with ESKAS closing in and time running out, Horan must choose between loyalty to his species and compassion for the fragile world he has come to love.

## The World of ESKAS

The novel is set in a future where civilizations rise beyond the limits of conventional imagination. ESKAS is a Type V civilization — a species that has mastered time itself and built its power around the control of reality.

In such a world, ordinary morality breaks down. Entire planets are sacrificed in the name of progress. Resources are treated like divine currency. And the true conflict is not just between species, but between the idea of survival and the cost of survival.
