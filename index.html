Chapter 1

The Beginning of Horan

I was not born under this sky. I do not belong to this fragile world of dust and breath.

I come from ESKAS, a civilization that does not merely live in time but bends it, reshapes it, commands it. We are what lesser minds would call a Type V civilization—architects of reality.

And yet, even gods can starve.

At the heart of ESKAS lies Exlir, the pulse of our existence—the mineral that fuels our dominion over time and matter. It is ancient, unforgiving, and rare.

So we began the hunt.

World after world, galaxy after galaxy, we searched. We calculated. We conquered. And when a planet failed to yield even a trace of Exlir, we erased it.

Until the day I failed.

I was sent to a world teeming with life, a world that laughed, dreamed, struggled just like this one. I walked among them unseen, studying their skies, their soil, their souls.

I reported nothing. I delayed the inevitable. But ESKAS does not tolerate hesitation. They came. And they burned the world to silence.

That day, something inside me fractured. Now I am no longer a seeker. I am an executioner, sent from planet to planet carrying a single command: find Exlir.

And then I found this place.

At first, it seemed ordinary. But something beneath the surface called to me. Not Exlir—not exactly. For the first time since my exile, I felt hope.

Am I here to save my civilization or to destroy another innocent world?

Because if this planet holds the key to Exlir, then ESKAS will come. And when they do, time itself will not be enough to save it.
